A simulation is a stubborn argument with physics — I spend my days losing it carefully.

I'm an FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, currently at DNEG Sydney; previously Fin Design + Effects. I make the destruction and the weather for feature film.

Before VFX, I studied interaction design and creative technology across four continents — Parsons, USyd, LMU Munich, KAIST and Aalto — which is why I'd rather template an effect than build it twice.
