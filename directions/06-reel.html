A simulation is a stubborn argument with physics — I spend my days losing it carefully, so grains, smoke and broken things behave like they belong on a screen the size of a building.

I'm an FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, currently at DNEG Sydney; previously Fin Design + Effects. Before VFX, I studied interaction design, creative technology and HCI — which is why I'd rather template an effect than build it twice.

Born in Poland, raised and based in Sydney, with study and work that's taken me across four continents.
