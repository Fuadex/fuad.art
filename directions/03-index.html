§02 — ABOUT

A simulation is a stubborn argument with physics. I lose it carefully, on purpose.

FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, currently at DNEG Sydney; previously Fin Design + Effects. I make the destruction and the weather: sandstorms, snowfall, breaking glass, dust, fire, sparks.

Before VFX: interaction design, creative technology and HCI across three continents. I template what I'd otherwise build twice, and I'm happiest with my hands in a solver.
