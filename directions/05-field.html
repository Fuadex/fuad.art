About

// whoami

A simulation is a stubborn argument with physics — I spend my days losing it carefully.

I'm an FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, currently at DNEG Sydney; previously Fin Design + Effects. I make the destruction and the weather: sandstorms, snowfall, breaking glass, dust, fire and sparks for feature film.

Before VFX, I studied interaction design, creative technology and HCI across three continents — Parsons, the University of Sydney, and LMU Munich. That background is why I'd rather build a templated asset than the same effect twice, and why a page like this one is just another solver to me.
