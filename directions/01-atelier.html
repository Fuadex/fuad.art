About

A simulation is just a stubborn argument with physics. I spend my days losing it carefully — coaxing grains, smoke and broken things into behaving like they belong on a screen the size of a building.

I'm an FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, currently at DNEG Sydney. Before that, Fin Design + Effects. Before that, three universities across three continents — Parsons, the University of Sydney, and LMU in Munich — studying interaction design, creative technology, and how to make machines feel a little less mechanical.

The films pay the bills. The art is in the in-between: the templated assets, the deformer solvers, the wedge that finally settles after a hundred siblings.

"Created a Sandstorm simulation asset: multiple layers of Ground and Sky Volumes, Sandsnakes and Windlips particulates." — Furiosa Pipeline, 2023
"Templated a layered cloth and an intricate set of hundreds of connected wires." — M3GAN, 2022
