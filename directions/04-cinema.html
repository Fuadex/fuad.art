About

A simulation is a stubborn argument with physics —
I spend my days losing it carefully, so that grains, smoke and broken things behave like they belong on a screen the size of a building.

I'm an FX Technical Director with five years in Houdini, shaped across DNEG Sydney and Fin Design + Effects. Before VFX, I studied interaction design and creative technology across four continents — Parsons New York, KAIST Korea, Aalto Helsinki and LMU Munich. I template what I'd otherwise build twice, and I'm happiest with my hands in a solver.
